# MidJourney

## 提示词示例

```
minimalism, full view, highly detailed, surrealism, high detail, dynamic lighting, dynamic shadows, minimalism, for web design business, behance, white DJI drones, high quality product rendering, product lighting, modern industrial products, streamlined --ar 16:9 --stylize 1000 --v 4 --v 5
```

## 参数说明

- `--ar 16:9` 设置16:9的画幅比例
- `--stylize 1000` 设置风格化程度为1000
- `--v 4` / `--v 5` 使用版本4或5
